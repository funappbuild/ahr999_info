How it works

The app has one capacity: scheduled alerts + a suggestion computed from the current Ahr999 index.

1

Choose your schedule

Pick daily, weekly, or monthly — and set the exact time you want the alert.

2

Choose your trigger

Receive alerts when: (i) any Ahr999 value, (ii) bottom zone only, or (iii) fixed investment zone only.

3

Get the suggestion

Each notification includes today’s Ahr999 and a clear suggestion: Buy the Bottom, Fixed Investment, or Pause.

Suggestion zones

You can adjust thresholds inside your app. Below is a simple, transparent model for illustration.

Buy the Bottom ≤ 0.45

Deep value conditions — A more aggressive accumulation stance.

Fixed Investment 0.45–1.20

DCA-style zone — steady, disciplined investing may be appropriate.

Pause ≥ 1.20

Overheated conditions — Reducing or pausing purchases.

Note: Ahr999 isn’t a guarantee. The app provides a simple signal based solely on the current index.

FAQ

Short answers to common concerns.

Does the app do anything besides alerts + suggestions?

No — the app’s sole capacity is scheduled notifications and a simple suggestion computed from the current Ahr999 index.

What data do you collect?

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand basic app usage and country/region distribution. No personal data or device identifiers are collected. See our privacy policy for details.

Is this financial advice?

No. It’s an informational signal based on a single index, presented as a simple suggestion.
